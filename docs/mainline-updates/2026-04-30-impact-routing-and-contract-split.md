## Why This Update Exists

The contract previously listed 13 commands in a flat `requiredCommands` array with no signal that some were the seven mandatory delivery stages and others were auxiliaries. Constitution Section 2 makes the layering explicit, but the runtime contract did not — so `speckit.version` looked like a peer of `speckit.readiness`. This patch closes that semantic gap.

Two changeTypes were missing from impact routing:
- `worktree_parity_change`: deep-review found that `docs/project-worktree-parity-governance.md` is classified as `source_of_truth` in `documentAuthority`, but no route existed to fan its changes out to the bootstrap and init scripts that must implement the rules. The April 28 worktree-agents-md-parity batch exposed this gap in retrospect.
- `readiness_change`: readiness re-classification has downstream effects on plan / tasks / intent-ledger / ECI dossier that were governed only by prose, not routing.

Direct edits to `.claude/agents/*.md` are silently overwritten on the next seed-claude-agents run. Constitution Section 12 classifies these as `dependent`, but until now there was no in-file warning to alert someone who might otherwise spend effort editing the wrong layer.
